## 5. Operating rules

- **Single source of truth:** this document plus the Report 2 registers; updates after each sync, with decisions logged on the sync meeting page.
- **Escalation path:** P0/P1 register items → next PD meeting immediately (CN items via G-PIE routing); P2/P3 → monthly sync. No item leaves a meeting without an owner and a due date.
- **Review checkpoint:** after two sync sessions, review cadence (monthly vs bi-monthly), the page-macro pilot result, and whether CP deliverable proposals (actions 6–8) have an approved adoption path; adjust this plan accordingly.
- **Document maintenance:** Eric updates the action tracker status column after each sync; superseded actions are marked Closed with a one-line disposition rather than deleted.
